About Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana

A government-backed savings scheme for eligible girl children.

🎯 Purpose

Helps families build a savings habit for an eligible girl child's future needs, such as education and other important milestones.

🏦 Account Opening

Parents or guardians can contact an authorised bank or India Post office to learn about eligibility, documents and the account-opening process.

📘 Official Rules

Check official sources for the latest interest rate, deposit limits, eligibility and withdrawal conditions.

Our Digital Awareness Initiative

Making scheme information clear, accessible and easy to understand.

🌐 Digital Awareness

Share simple explanations in English and Hindi so more families can understand the scheme.

📍 Account Guidance

Help families find reliable guidance from official sources and authorised banks or post offices.

📊 Survey-Based Action

Use village survey responses to identify topics where people need clearer information.

Expected benefit: This website aims to make basic SSY information easier to understand and access. It is an educational prototype and cannot open accounts or process applications.

Steps to Learn More About SSY

Confirm the current rules with an official source before taking action.

1. Check Eligibility

Check the official eligibility conditions and scheme guidelines.

2. Visit an Authorised Institution

Visit an authorised bank or India Post office to ask about forms, documents and the application procedure.

3. Confirm the Rules

Confirm current deposit rules, applicable interest rate and withdrawal conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can explain the account-opening process?

An authorised bank or India Post office can provide information about eligibility, documents and the current procedure.

Why was this website created?

It was created as a Community Development Project to share simple information about SSY and support awareness after a village survey.

Is this an official government website?

No. SSY Saathi is an educational Community Development Project website.
